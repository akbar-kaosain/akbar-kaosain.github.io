Overview

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, where I work in software engineering under the supervision of Prof. Mahmoud Alfadel.

Before transitioning into software engineering, my research focused on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning for energy systems, particularly energy disaggregation. I worked with supervised and semi-supervised learning, deep-learning models for time-series data, electric-vehicle charging data, forecasting, imputation, and related applied AI methods.

I completed my master's degree in Systems Engineering at Concordia University in Montreal, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Nizar Bouguila and co-supervision of Prof. Manar Amayri. Prior to that, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in Bangladesh, where my final capstone project was supervised by Prof. Nova Ahmed.
