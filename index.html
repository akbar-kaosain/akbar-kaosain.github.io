Overview

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, where I conduct research in software engineering under the supervision of Prof. Mahmoud Alfadel.

Before moving into software engineering, my research was primarily focused on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning for energy systems, particularly energy disaggregation. My work has involved supervised and semi-supervised learning, time-series modelling, electric-vehicle charging data, forecasting, data imputation, and other applied AI methods.

I completed my master's degree in Systems Engineering at Concordia University in Montreal, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Nizar Bouguila and Prof. Manar Amayri. I previously earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in Bangladesh, where my final capstone project was supervised by Prof. Nova Ahmed.
